# Proposed Preprocessing Plan

> **Scope note:** This document preserves the earlier Biopsy-classification proposal as an alternative planning direction. The subsequent provisional experiments use K-Means and DBSCAN clustering as documented in `clustering_study_plan.md` and the experiment reports. Neither direction is presented as teacher-approved; the classification steps below were not implemented in the current workflow.

## Scope and current evidence

This document records a proposal prepared before implementation. At that stage it did not change the raw CSV, create processed data, train models, or make predictions. The inspected file is `data/raw/risk_factors_cervical_cancer.csv` (858 rows, 36 columns). The current provisional clustering preprocessing and experiments are documented in their generated audit and experiment reports; they do not imply that the options in this earlier classification proposal were approved or implemented.

The inspection report records 23 exact duplicate rows, literal `?` values in feature columns, no missing values in the four screening outcomes, and a `Biopsy` distribution of 803 negative (0) and 55 positive (1) observations. The two diagnosis-time fields each have 787 missing values.

## Investigation of diagnosis-time missingness

There is evidence for a possible structural explanation, but not enough to assign a reason to every missing value:

- In this CSV, the two diagnosis-time fields are missing together in 787 rows; both are present in the other 71 rows.
- All 787 rows where the time fields are missing have `STDs: Number of diagnosis = 0`. All 71 rows with recorded times have at least one recorded STD diagnosis (68 have count 1, two have count 2, and one has count 3).
- The CSV also has eight rows with `STDs = 1` but missing times, and 105 rows with missing STD status and missing times. Those cases cannot be explained as confirmed “no diagnosis” from these fields alone.
- UCI says that some patients declined to answer questions because of privacy concerns ([UCI dataset page](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/383/cervical%2Bcancer%2Brisk%2Bfactors)). A study using this dataset says most patients did not provide the two diagnosis-time values, possibly due to privacy concerns or lack of information ([study, discussion of dataset fields](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/134971/1/Manuscript_TFSC_SI_20200713_deposit.pdf), section 4.1).

Therefore, treat “no recorded STD diagnosis” as a plausible reason for many missing times, not as a confirmed meaning for every `?`. Do not convert these missing values to zero without checking the field definitions and obtaining teacher approval. The source’s privacy and unknown-information explanations also mean that missingness may be a mixture of structural absence and nonresponse.

## Biopsy imbalance and evaluation

`Biopsy` has 55 positive cases (6.4%) and 803 negative cases (93.6%). An all-negative prediction would be 93.6% accurate but have zero recall for positive cases. Therefore, report accuracy only with class-sensitive measures: confusion matrix, positive-class recall/sensitivity, precision, specificity, F1, and balanced accuracy. Keep splits stratified and state the exact split counts. With only 55 positives, metrics can vary substantially with the split. Any resampling must be confined to training data and requires teacher approval; do not resample the test set.

## Leakage review for a Biopsy target

A defensible initial experiment would ask: “Can the recorded demographic, habit, and prior-history features predict the Biopsy label, without using other screening outcomes or diagnosis fields whose timing is uncertain?” Under that question:

- Exclude `Hinselmann`, `Schiller`, and `Citology` from predictors by default. They are parallel screening outcomes and may reveal information closely tied to the biopsy assessment.
- Exclude `Dx:Cancer`, `Dx:CIN`, `Dx:HPV`, and `Dx` until source definitions and timing establish that each is known before the biopsy target. Their names suggest diagnosis variables, but the CSV and inspection report do not establish when they were recorded.
- Treat diagnosis-time values and `STDs: Number of diagnosis` as history fields only after confirming their temporal meaning. Do not use Biopsy itself or any outcome-derived field as an input.
- Candidate baseline predictors are the demographic/habit/history fields listed in Step 11. Final inclusion requires a clear prediction-time definition and teacher approval.

## Decisions requiring teacher approval

1. Whether the 23 exact duplicate rows are true duplicate records and the removal or grouping policy.
2. Whether to exclude the two diagnosis-time columns, keep them missing-aware, or use a justified structural-missing treatment.
3. The missing-value imputation method, including whether to add missingness indicators.
4. The intended prediction time and final Biopsy predictor set, especially for `Dx*`, `Dx`, STD diagnosis-history, and other screening outcome fields.
5. Whether any class resampling or supplementary Apriori experiment is expected.
6. The train/test or validation scheme and required evaluation measures for the college submission.

## Syllabus and project coverage summary

- **Data collection:** document the UCI source and retain the supplied raw file unchanged.
- **Preprocessing:** investigate duplicates, parse `?`, review missingness and types, and apply only justified imputation/transform/scaling/sampling on a derived dataset.
- **Visualization:** inspect missingness, distributions, and class balance.
- **Data mining technique:** prioritize syllabus-based Naive Bayes, KNN, or Decision Tree classification for the provisional Biopsy target; Apriori is an optional separate analysis. K-Means and DBSCAN are not selected for the primary task.
- **Experiments, results, analysis, conclusion:** compare approved syllabus models under consistent stratified evaluation, report minority-class metrics, discuss limitations, and give an educational conclusion.

This project is educational and is not intended for clinical diagnosis.
